## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies
The paper comes out of Math Brain, an ongoing project on the use of AI systems for research mathematics, and its results were developed with the Brain and Math Brain. In November 2026, Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) and a team of Codex agents running OpenAI's GPT-6.1, all connected to the Brain, retrieved the spectral-level entropy lemma and related methods from the author's earlier work through Math Brain, sharpened the level-entropy bound with an interval certificate, derived the split of the entropy into level and within-level parts and the noise-stability estimate, proved the Bessel-function inequalities and the asymptotic restriction estimate, carried out the finite computations, and searched the literature. Separate agents refereed the proof of the main theorem and of the asymptotic restriction estimate. Claude Opus 5.5 coordinated the agents, assembled the proofs, prepared the manuscript, and audited it, including the proofs, the numerical constants and the finite computations. These checks are not peer review. The author directed the work and is responsible for the content of the paper and for decisions concerning its dissemination.
